Professional Overview

I am an Ottawa-based business and financial analyst focused on improving operational efficiency, process optimization, and generating data-driven actionable insights. I hold a strong background in Commerce and Business Administration to continuously build on my technical skills in accounting, business intelligence, and data analytics. I apply these analytical methods to support management decision-making in a multifunctional and collaborative environment.

My technical expertise combines with advanced data visualization tools like Power BI and Tableau, and foundational programming analysis using SQL and Python, and structured process improvement methodologies such as Lean Six Sigma. By incorporating technical analysis with financial theories and operational metrics, I seek to assist the organizations and teams to identify operational bottlenecks, refine processes and resource allocation, and improve business outcomes.

To polish my analytical capabilities, I am taking the Business Intelligence & Data Analyst (BIDA) certification, along with maintaining the Canadian Securities Course (CSC), and the QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor credential to ensure I can excel in this Business Operations and Process Improvement role.
